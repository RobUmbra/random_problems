[MPFG_2021_2]

[MPFG_2021_5]

[MPFG_2021_8]
How many three-digit integers have digits that are strictly increasing from left to right? (For example, $137$ and $459$ qualify, but $334$ and $752$ do not.)

[MPFG_2021_12]

[MPFG_2021_18]

[MPFG_2022_9]

[MPFG_2022_14]

[MPFG_2023_8]

[MPFG_2024_17]
